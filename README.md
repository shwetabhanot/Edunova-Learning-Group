# 🏫 Company Background: Edunova Learning Group

**Industry:** Education & Training  
**Size:** ~500 employees  
**Headquarters:** Melbourne, Australia  
**Delivery Modes:** In-person, Online, Hybrid  
**Annual Students Served:** ~12,000  
**Campuses:** 3 locations across Victoria and South Australia

---

## 📘 About Edunova

*Edunova Learning Group* is a mid-sized education provider delivering accredited vocational education and corporate training programs. With government funding, direct student enrollments, and B2B partnerships, Edunova is committed to high-impact learning with flexible delivery.

Their core revenue streams include:
- Government-funded programs (Skills First & JobTrainer)
- Student tuition and certifications
- Corporate short courses and leadership training
- Online modules through their digital platform

## 🧠 My Role: FP&A Analyst

As an FP&A Analyst, I support senior leadership and departmental managers with data-driven financial insights. My reports help:
- Monitor variances in actual vs budgeted spend
- Forecast revenue from student enrollments
- Track performance by cost centre and department
- Analyze payroll and workforce diversity trends

---

## 📁 Projects in This Repository

Each folder contains Excel dashboards and Power BI mockups built around Edunova’s internal FP&A needs.

1. **Budget vs Actual**
   - Monthly financial variances across academic departments
   - Highlights areas over/under budget

2. **Sales Forecasting**
   - Forecast vs actual revenue from courses and certifications
   - Split by region, delivery mode, and course category

3. **Cost Centre Performance**
   - Financial accountability of support teams (IT, HR, Facilities)
   - Departmental cost trends and efficiency metrics

4. **Headcount and Payroll**
   - Payroll and workforce composition across roles
   - Monthly and gender-based workforce analysis

---

Each report helps leadership make smarter, data-informed decisions to support Edunova’s growth and mission in education.
